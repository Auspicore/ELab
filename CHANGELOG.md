# ELab Release History

[简体中文](#zh-cn) · [Product overview](README.md) · [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/Auspicore/ELab/releases)

## Documentation v1.1.0 — Workflows and first experiment

- The product overview explains the sequence from drawing a control circuit through simulation, observation, and review.
- The quick start describes a motor start/stop exercise, what to observe at each step, and how to interpret the results.
- The download guide lists all six target architectures and explains how to choose and verify a package once one is published.
- The release includes the bilingual offline HTML guide and its SHA-256 checksum. [Release notes and assets](https://github.com/Auspicore/ELab/releases/tag/docs-v1.1.0)

## Documentation v1.0.0 — Bilingual product introduction

- Introduced the English and Simplified Chinese product pages, platform status, quick start, and user feedback routes.
- Included the installation and checksum guide, FAQ, and private vulnerability reporting instructions. [Release notes and assets](https://github.com/Auspicore/ELab/releases/tag/docs-v1.0.0)

## Desktop applications

Windows, Linux, and macOS releases are in preparation. Desktop release notes will identify the tested systems, matching installer assets, checksums, and known limitations.

- 附件包含双语离线 HTML 指南及 SHA-256 校验文件。[版本说明与附件](https://github.com/Auspicore/ELab/releases/tag/docs-v1.1.0)
