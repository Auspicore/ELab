### Can I move a project between Windows, Linux, and macOS?

Project exchange across platforms is pending verification. When available, the release notes will describe compatible versions and any required steps.

### What can I try for free?

Free evaluation terms and the scope of paid features are pending announcement.

### What can I observe in a simulation?

In the motor start/stop experiment, you can follow the contactor and motor through start, self-holding, stop, and overload trip. Review the event timeline to compare operating sequences. The guide includes expected results you can read before a public download is available. [First experiment](quick-start.md)

### How do I check a schematic before running it?

Run design validation and inspect each reported drawing or device. For a multi-sheet design, use cross-references to follow related device symbols and check your terminal connections before starting a live run.

### What can I share after a design review or experiment?

Export a schematic as SVG and export engineering lists such as devices, terminals, and connections as CSV. From a case session, export the project and its run and test records together for review.

## 常见问题

[产品介绍](../README.zh-CN.md)

### 现在可以下载 ELab 吗？

公开下载待开放。请查看[下载 ELab](download.md#zh-cn)了解平台状态。

### 工程可以在 Windows、Linux 和 macOS 间交换吗？

跨平台工程交换待验证。完成验证后，发行说明会列明兼容版本及所需操作。

### 可以免费体验哪些功能？

免费体验条款和付费功能范围待公布。

### 仿真中可以观察什么？

在起保停实验中，可观察启动、自保持、停止和过载脱扣过程中的接触器与电动机状态，也可查看事件时间线，对照不同操作顺序。公开下载开放前，可以先阅读实验指南中的预期结果。[第一个实验](quick-start.md#zh-cn)

### 如何在仿真前检查原理图？

运行设计校验，根据结果查看相关图纸或器件。设计包含多张图纸时，可通过跨页引用找到同一设备的相关符号，在启动仿真前检查端子的连接。

### 设计评审或实验结束后可以分享哪些成果？

可以将原理图导出为 SVG，将设备表、端子表、连接表等工程清单导出为 CSV。还可从案例会话中导出工程、运行记录和测试结果，供评审或复盘使用。
